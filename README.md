## ⚡ 5 Forensic Detection Engines Working for You

When you load your general ledger or Accounts Payable (AP) export into **The Audit Shield**, it runs standard mathematical and behavioral tests designed by forensic accounting experts:

### 1. 📊 Benford’s Law Digit Analysis

Human-fabricated numbers are rarely random. When fraudsters invent invoice amounts or manipulate ledger entries, they tend to over-use specific digits.
The Audit Shield applies logarithmic frequency modeling across 1st and 2nd digits using Benford's Law equation:

$$P(d) = \log_{10}\left(1 + \frac{1}{d}\right)$$

It instantly identifies statistically impossible digit distributions, flagging manipulated ledgers before you even start substantive testing.

### 2. 🔍 Split Transaction & Approval Limit Evader

If a manager has a $10,000 approval limit, a fraudulent transaction or vendor split won't show up at $12,000. It will show up as three invoices at **$9,850, $9,900, and $9,750** within a 48-hour window.
The engine scans transaction clusters to reveal systematic threshold avoidance automatically.

### 3. 👯 Fuzzy Duplicate Payment & Ghost Vendor Finder

Vendor names change slightly across batches: *"Acme Consulting LLC"* vs. *"Acme Consulting Inc."*
Standard VLOOKUP fails here. The Audit Shield employs multi-attribute fuzzy matching across vendor names, payment dates, and amounts to pinpoint hidden duplicate disbursements.

### 4. 📅 Temporal & Off-Hours Anomaly Detector

Why was a manual journal entry posted at **2:14 AM on a Sunday** right before quarter-end closing?
The tool automatically tags off-hours, weekend, and holiday overrides—the classic breeding grounds for unapproved adjustments.

### 5. 📈 Statistical Outlier & Z-Score Engine

By running Z-Score and Interquartile Range (IQR) analyses across expense categories, the system isolates statistical outliers that fall 3+ standard deviations away from the baseline norm.
